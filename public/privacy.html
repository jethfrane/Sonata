Sonata

Privacy & terms

Last updated 13 November 2026

  • Sonata has no accounts. Your songs are saved on your own device.
  • Nothing you write is sent anywhere unless you choose to back it up or share it.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold.

Privacy

Your songs and settings

Songs, setlists, settings and earlier versions of your library are stored in your browser on the device you are using. Sonata's servers do not receive or keep them. Clearing the browser's site data, or removing the installed app, deletes them, so keep a backup.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Sonata asks for two things: your email address, to show which account is connected, and permission to use files that Sonata itself creates in your Google Drive. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

  • Your library is saved to a file in your own Drive. It goes straight from your browser to Google; it does not pass through or get stored on Sonata's servers.
  • Your name, email address and profile picture are kept on your device only, to show the connected account.
  • Google data is used only to back up and restore your library and to publish what you choose to share. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not given to anyone else.
  • To disconnect, sign out in Backup. You can also remove Sonata's access in your Google Account under Security, and delete the Sonata files from your Drive.

What you share

  • Song and setlist links and QR codes. The chart is packed into the link itself. Anyone who has the link can read that chart. When a link is opened, the server reads it once to show a title in the link preview and does not store it.
  • Song Bank and live setlists. When you publish one, Sonata creates a file in your Google Drive that anyone with its link can view. Sonata's server fetches that file from Google each time the link is opened and keeps no copy. Stop sharing at any time and the link stops working.

Importing from a web link

When you paste a link to a chord page, Sonata's server fetches that public page, passes the chart text back to you and stores nothing. To prevent abuse, the number of requests from one network address is counted for about a minute and then forgotten.

Microphone

The tuner listens through your microphone only while it is on, and only after you allow it. The sound is analysed on your device. It is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Reference videos

If you add a YouTube link to a song, the video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player only when you press play. YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply to that video.

Visit counts and hosting

Sonata uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It uses no cookies and does not identify you. Sonata is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes technical details such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Children

Sonata does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Signing in with Google is subject to Google's own age requirements.
